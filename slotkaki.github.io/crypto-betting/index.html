From crypto deposit to a placed bet

  1. Deposit USDT, BTC or ETH at the counter, on the network shown there. The full checklist is in the crypto deposit guide.
  2. Wait until the deposit is credited to your ringgit balance.
  3. Open the sports section, choose a match and a market, and check the odds shown on the bet slip.
  4. Enter your stake and read the slip once more before confirming, because a placed bet usually cannot be changed.
  5. Follow open bets in your bet history. Settled returns go back to the same ringgit balance.

Turnover and withdrawals after betting

Turnover is the total amount you have staked. Each deposit has to be wagered at least once before it can be withdrawn, and sports bets count towards that total like any other bet. Winning or losing does not change the count; the stake does.

When you withdraw, the payout is sent in the coin you deposited, to the address and network you enter. Check both twice before submitting.

Keep sports betting within limits

Decide your stake per match before kick-off and keep a deposit limit in your account. Live betting moves quickly, so a pause between bets helps you stay within the plan. Do not chase losses with bigger stakes.

Common crypto betting problems

My crypto deposit has not reached the sports section

Every section uses the same ringgit balance. If the balance has not changed, check the TxID and the network first; the steps are in the crypto deposit guide.

A bet was voided

The reason is shown in your bet history, and a voided stake normally returns to your balance. Ask support if the reason is unclear.

The odds changed before I confirmed

Odds move with the match. If the slip shows new odds, read them before you confirm again.
