How to deposit USDT, BTC or ETH

  1. Log in on slotkaki.org, open the Deposit counter and choose the coin.
  2. Copy the address and note the network shown for this request. Never reuse an address from an earlier deposit.
  3. In your wallet, choose exactly the same network. This matters most for USDT, which exists on more than one network, such as TRC20 and ERC20.

Common crypto deposit problems

Sent on the wrong network

Contact support at once with the TxID, the receiving address, the coin and the network you used. Recovery may not be possible and cannot be promised, so test with a small amount next time.

Sent, but the balance has not changed

Blockchain transfers need network confirmations before they are credited. Look up the TxID in a block explorer. If it is confirmed and the counter shows nothing, send the TxID to support.

The pasted address does not match

Do not send. Clear the clipboard, copy again from the counter, and scan your device for malware if the mismatch happens again.

Someone offers to "convert" crypto for you

That is not part of the deposit process. Deposit only from your own wallet to the address shown at the counter.
